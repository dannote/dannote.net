→ Bring your codebase to Make
→ Edit code with precise changes
→ Branch, commit, ship</.x_post>

In my opinion, that’s the point where they’ll get stuck.

The next step requires proper abstractions for bidirectional design-to-code mapping.

And all the vibe-coding platforms like Replit and Lovable are already hitting the limits of project scale because they generate unmanaged and unmaintainable AI-slop backends.

That’s why I feel comfortable building a competitor to all of them, solo and in the open.
